What your catalog now forbids

Survivor-only panel. So you may not write:

"The average name in my panel returned X%."

Not "with a caveat." Not at all.

The limitations you wrote last week are a constraint on what you may say this week.

Three honest responses

  1. Fix the input — rebuild from point-in-time membership
  2. Bound the damage — estimate the contamination and report the bound
  3. Restrict the claim — say only what the panel supports

All three are professional.

There is no fourth option where you compute it, know what is wrong, and report it plainly.

The catalog exists so there is a written record at the moment a deadline tempts you.
