Week 4 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

Constraints are declared before results are seen

Your constraints go in writing during prework, before you optimize. In Lab 4 Step 6 you will be asked whether you chose them before or after seeing the weights, and the honest answer is required.

Choosing constraints after seeing an ugly result is legitimate practice — but it must be disclosed as what it is. Presenting a post-hoc constraint as a prior principle is not.

Every remedy names its cost

Constraints, shrinkage, minimum variance, risk parity — each gives something up. A remedy reported without its cost is incomplete.

In-sample results are labelled as such

Any Sharpe ratio, return, or volatility computed on the same data used to estimate the inputs is labelled in-sample, every time it appears.

An unlabelled performance number is read as out-of-sample. If it is not, that is a misrepresentation, and it is the most common one in this field.

Out-of-sample means untouched

A portfolio tested out of sample is estimated on one period and held, unchanged, through another. No re-estimation, no rebalancing, no parameter tweaks informed by the test period.

If you rebalance during the test, say so and say on what rule — that is a different and legitimate experiment, but it is not the one described here.

Leverage is reported, always

Every portfolio you report carries its gross leverage — the sum of absolute weights. A weight vector without it hides the most important thing about it.

Inherited conventions

Your Week 3 conventions bind here: return type, annualization basis, risk-free assumption, window. The optimizer inherits every one of them silently.

If a convention changes, every downstream number changes with it. Say so.

AI use

AI writes optimizers readily and writes them correctly. It will not tell you that the output is unstable, will not check the second window, and will not label anything in-sample.

Ask for the code. Then do the diagnostic work yourself — that is what this week is.

What must be yours: the constraint choices, the diagnosis, the remedy selection and its stated cost, and the judgment about whether the result is a statement about the world or about your sample.

You must be able to explain every line of code you submit.

Reproducibility and citation

Unchanged: fresh-kernel runs, every number traceable to code, every source in source_log.md.
