  7. Your source_log.md, covering every source both labs used: the French library, the Yahoo quote page, and any WRDS table.
  8. Your AI disclosure: each tool and how it was used, in the markdown files or as a separate note.

Markdown, notebooks and data files, as the labs produced them. No PDF or Word conversion.

How it is read

The lab pages are the rubric. Each item above is graded against the step it comes from, on the lab's own terms: conventions stated, numbers traceable to code, verification against something you did not write, defences that answer or concede, findings with evidence. A defended departure from the method earns full marks, as the labs say.

A notebook without visible executed output is read as unfinished. Two sources minimum, one professional where you have access; a documented failed attempt counts, the outcome never does.
