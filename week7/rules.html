MSF Financial Modeling

Week 7 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

The clock is fixed

Four minutes, then you are stopped. Two minutes of questions. Fourteen defences share a ninety-minute session, and the clock is the only thing protecting the last presenter.

One slide

The exhibit. A deck is not submitted and not projected. Your notebook may be open for the questions.

Unaided

No AI in the room during a defence, not on your screen and not in your ear. No script read aloud. Notes on one card are fine.

This is the one assessment in this course that is invigilated, and the point of it is to find out whether the understanding is real. Everything else you submit is AI-assisted by policy; this is not.

The score is written before the feedback

The defence is scored against the five-part structure at the moment it happens. Feedback comes after, in writing. They are separate acts and they stay separate.

Feedback names defects, not fixes

Written comments in Brightspace within twelve hours of your presenting day, against the same structure for everyone. They say what is missing or unsupported. They do not say what to put there. The fix is yours, which is what keeps the work yours.

You attend both days

You present on one and act as assigned discussant on the other. The discussant question is submitted in writing before the session. The discussant record is collected in the room.

Discussants are graded on their own work only

The quality of your question and your record of the answer. Never on the presenter's performance, and your question never affects their score.

A missed defence is arranged before, not after

The defence is in person and cannot be repeated for one student without the room. If you cannot attend your assigned day, arrange the alternative before that day through the instructor; an accessible equivalent exists and is arranged where required. A defence missed without arrangement cannot be reconstructed after the term ends.

The capstone objects are submitted after both defence days

The model, the investment-committee memo and the presentation video are due after the last defence, so the room's questions can reach them. Their dates are on their own Brightspace pages.

The exhibit's prices are dated within a week

Every market price on the exhibit carries its date and source, and the date is within seven days of submission. A stale price makes every gap on the page wrong.

Both models were built the same way

The comparison is only defensible if the horizon, the terminal convention and the discount-rate construction match, with only the beta and the leverage differing. Expect to be asked.

Edition A is not rewritten

It is quoted as written, including what it got wrong. A revised Edition A is not an Edition A.

The revision section is required and is specific

What the defence exposed, what changed with numbers before and after, and what you were asked to change and did not, with the reason. "Incorporated helpful feedback" is not a revision section.

AI use

Disclosed as always: each tool and what it was used for, in the memo and in the notebook. Undisclosed use is the one integrity failure this course treats as serious.

During the defence itself, none.

Reproducibility and citation

Unchanged: fresh-kernel runs, every number traceable, every source in source_log.md with access route, coverage window, and retrieval date. A number you cannot locate in your own notebook, in the room, is treated as unsupported.
