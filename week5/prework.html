Part C — Two companies, screened

Thursday's checkpoint locks the two public companies you will value for the capstone. Come with a shortlist, already checked, because a company that cannot be valued cannot be swapped out later without costing you a session.

Change the pick if any of these is true:

Why it breaks
It does not file a 10-K with the SEC a foreign private issuer files a 20-F on a different schedule; a private company files nothing
It is a bank, an insurer, or a REIT their balance sheets and cash flows do not work the way a projection treats them, and the method you learn this week does not apply
It has no revenue, or no operating income in at least three of the last five years a projection forecasts a business that exists
It closed a transformational merger in the last two years its history is two different companies, and every growth rate spans the seam

Then check you can reach the numbers, which takes two minutes each. Send this to your AI partner with the CIK filled in:

Pull company facts from https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK{ten-digit CIK}.json with a User-Agent header that names me. Print revenue, operating income and total assets for the last three fiscal years from the 10-K observations, with the accession number for each, and print each fiscal year's start date, end date and length in days.

If three years come back for both companies, you are ready. If they do not, pick again tonight rather than discovering it next Tuesday.
