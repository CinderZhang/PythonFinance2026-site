MSF Financial Modeling

Week 5 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

Every input is dated and labelled

Each figure carries its source, the accession number or statement it came from, the date, and one of four labels: history, guidance, judgment, fact. An input you cannot date is recorded as undated, with a note saying what you would need to date it. Undated inputs are permitted. Silently omitted ones are not.

The five inputs are stated separately

The cash flows, the discount rate, the terminal growth rate, any bridge, and the share count. A valuation that does not show all five is not reviewable.

Cash is computed last

From the cash-flow statement: opening cash plus the period's flows. Never a plug. If you can type a number into cash, the model is wrong.

No balance, no valuation

Every projected year prints the balance identity, the cash tie between the balance sheet and the cash-flow statement, and cash against the floor. The valuation cell refuses to run when a check fails.

A balance sheet that does not balance is not a forecast. It is a bug.

The terminal guard

The model raises an error when terminal growth is at or above the discount rate. Without the guard the arithmetic returns a confident negative number for a profitable company, and you will not notice.

Terminal growth is below the long-run growth rate of the economy the company operates in.

The terminal share is reported with the horizon beside it

Neither means anything alone. Lengthening the forecast lowers the share without changing the value.

The discount rate is built, not typed

Risk-free rate, beta and equity risk premium, each stated. The beta is computed from returns, and its standard error is reported with it. Whatever you treat as debt in the cash flow, treat as debt in the rate.

The share count is the latest filed count

From the most recent 10-Q or 10-K cover, dated, labelled a fact. Not the year's weighted average, not a data feed's field.

Rerun, never add

Scenarios are rerun through the whole model. Sensitivities are never summed.

Ranges, not points

The valuation is reported as a range against a market price you dated, with the assumption most likely to move it named.

The target lock is binding

Two companies, screened, with the screen confirmed in writing. ABG is not eligible. A change after the lock goes through the instructor.

AI use

AI builds valuation models quickly and mostly correctly. It will also invent plausible inputs, will not know the vintage of anything, will produce a point estimate to two decimal places, and will read a standardised data field as though it were the filing.

Never accept a number from an AI into your model without a source for it. What must be yours: the assumptions and their labels, the vintage choices, the range, and the answer to what would move it. You must be able to explain every line of code and every input you submit.

Reproducibility and citation

Unchanged: fresh-kernel runs, every number traceable, every source in source_log.md with access route, coverage window, and retrieval date.
