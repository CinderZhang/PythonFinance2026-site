MSF Financial Modeling

Week 5 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

The policy is chosen before it is priced

Your rebalancing frequency goes in writing during prework, before you compute its cost. In Lab 5 Step 3 you will be asked whether you chose before or after seeing the table, and the honest answer is required.

Choosing after is legitimate practice. It must be disclosed as what it is. Presenting a post-hoc policy as a prior principle is not.

An alpha is reported with what produced the weights

Every alpha you report carries its t-statistic and one sentence on how the weights it was measured on were chosen. If the weights were fitted on the same window, the alpha is labelled in-sample, every time it appears.

An unlabelled alpha is read as a claim of skill. If that is not what you mean, label it.

A drawdown is reported with its dates and its convention

Every maximum drawdown you report carries the peak date, the trough date, the months to recover or "not recovered by window end", the return series it was computed on, and the observation frequency. A drawdown on monthly data is labelled as such: intramonth lows are not in it.

A past drawdown is a record of what happened. It is never reported as a limit on what can happen.

The cap is a cap on targets unless you say otherwise

The cap you defended in Week 4 binds your target weights. Holdings drift between resets. When you report a policy, report how far holdings drifted past the cap under it.

Costs are sourced, and tax is named

A round-trip cost you found is cited with its source. A round-trip cost you assumed is stated as an assumption, in those words. Tax is named as a cost that is not in the number.

Leverage is reported, always

Every portfolio you report carries its gross leverage. Inherited from Week 4; it does not lapse.

Inherited conventions

Your Week 3 and Week 4 conventions bind here: return type, frequency, annualization, risk-free source, window, and fixed weights reset monthly for the return series. The regression inherits every one of them. If a convention changes, every coefficient changes with it. Say so.

AI use

AI writes factor regressions and drawdown code fluently. Four things it will do without comment: report the alpha as evidence of skill; report a drawdown without its dates or its recovery; leave tax out of a trading-cost estimate and not say so; and describe a factor tilt as if the premium were guaranteed. It will not label anything in-sample.

Ask for the code. Then do the reading yourself. That is what this week is.

What must be yours: the three committee sentences, the before-or-after statement, the reading of the drawdown, the sheet, and the alpha defence.

You must be able to explain every line of code you submit.
