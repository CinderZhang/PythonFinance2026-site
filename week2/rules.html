Week 2 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

The Blocker rule

A Blocker open in data_quality_issues.md means no headline result. Not a result with a caveat. No result.

This binds for the rest of the course. Week 3 will take your Week 2 limitations statement at face value, and so will every week after it.

Logging an issue is not clearing it.

Evidence standard

Every entry in the catalog carries counts and percentages. Qualitative descriptions of data problems are not evidence.

  • Not evidence: "some names are missing", "returns look noisy", "coverage is spotty"
  • Evidence: "3 of 12 requested names returned 0 rows (25%)", "returns exceed ±50% on 4 of

3,140 security-days (0.13%)"

If you suspect a problem, measure it. A suspicion you did not measure does not go in the catalog; it goes in your notes until you measure it.

Severity is yours

You assign severity. Two analysts can rate the same issue differently and both defend it, because severity depends on what the panel is for.

Declining to rate is the only indefensible option.

Provenance of claims

Distinguish, in writing, between:

  • Evidence you generated — you ran it, here is the output
  • Evidence you read — documented elsewhere, here is the citation

Both are legitimate. Presenting the second as the first is not. This applies to the documentation findings in prework Part C and to anything your AI tells you about a dataset.

AI use

AI will happily write panel-construction code that contains every contamination in this week's handout. It has no way to know where your name list came from or whether your sample spans your event.

What must be yours: the universe choice, the severity ratings, the lag policy, and the judgment about which contamination actually matters for your question.

You must be able to explain every line of code you submit.

Live-data drift

The demonstrations in this week's materials use live sources and will change over time. If your reproduction differs from the printed numbers, report the difference — do not silently adjust to match. A drifted demonstration is a genuine finding and is treated as one.

Reproducibility and citation

Unchanged from Week 1: fresh-kernel runs, every number traceable to code, every source recorded in source_log.md with its access route, coverage window, and retrieval date.

Your source log now covers a panel rather than one security. Every source in it gets an entry.
