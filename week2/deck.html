What CRSP records that free data cannot

When a security is delisted, holders receive something — liquidation, acquisition, residual claim, or total loss.

That final observation is often the most important one in the security's history.

Delisting return
CRSPDLRET, plus DLSTCD for why
Free lanethe rows are simply gone

This is why the university pays for WRDS. Not better prices — observations free data structurally cannot hold.

When the vendor changes the data first

WRDS Corporate Bond Returns truncates monthly returns at +100% before publication.

  • +150% is recorded as +100%
  • −90% is recorded as −90%

Asymmetric. Means understated, skewness distorted — worst exactly where distressed-bond research lives.

No flag column. The only way to know is the README.

Documented case, cited — not a pull we ran today.
