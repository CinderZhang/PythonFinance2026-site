Part A — Reproduce all three demonstrations

Run each of the three code blocks in handout.md Parts 1, 2, and 4 yourself.

These use live data. The printed outputs were verified when the handout was written and may have drifted. If your numbers differ, that is a finding — bring it to class. Do not assume you made a mistake, and do not assume the handout is right.

Record for each: what you ran, what you got, and whether it matched.

Part B — Choose your universe

Decide what your panel will cover — 8 to 15 securities. Write down three things:

  1. What universe, and why that one?
  2. Where will your list of names come from?
  3. Was that list constructed at the start of your sample period, or the end?

Bring the answer to 3 in writing. It is much harder to answer honestly after you have seen the data, which is exactly why you write it now.

Pick something you care about. You will be defending this choice, and a universe you chose because it was convenient is hard to defend.

Part C — Read a provider's documentation

Pick one data source you actually use. Find its documentation — data dictionary, README, methodology note, terms of use, whichever exists.

Find and write down one filtering, cleaning, or coverage rule you did not know about. Examples of what to look for: what gets excluded, how corporate actions are handled, what the coverage window is, whether any values are capped or adjusted, what the update schedule is.

If you genuinely cannot find any documentation, that is your finding — write down where you looked. A source with no discoverable methodology is itself a fact about the source, and it belongs in your source log.

Part D — Write one paragraph

Describe an analysis you could run where a survivorship-contaminated panel would give you an answer that looks completely reasonable and is badly wrong. Be specific about what the wrong answer would look like.

Keep it. You will compare it against your own panel in Lab 2.

Bring to the session: three reproduced demonstrations with your numbers, your universe choice with the three answers, one documentation finding, and this paragraph.
