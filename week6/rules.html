Week 6 — Rules and Conventions

Register: RULES. No teaching here — see handout.md.

Every input is dated

Each figure in your valuation carries an as-of date and a basis. An input you cannot date is recorded in the ledger as undated, with a note saying what you would need to date it.

Undated inputs are permitted. Silently omitted ones are not.

Vintage is stated

Where a figure has been revised, state which vintage you used — as first reported, as-reported current, or point-in-time — and why that choice fits your purpose.

Using one vintage for some inputs and another for others is a defect unless deliberate and disclosed.

Basis is stated

GAAP or non-GAAP. Fiscal or calendar. Every time.

A valuation built on adjusted figures and presented without saying so is misleading even when every number in it is accurate.

Period comparability

Before any growth rate, confirm the periods are the same length and cover the same season. A comparison spanning a 53-week year, a fiscal-year change, or a stub period is adjusted or flagged.

Ranges, not points

Valuations are reported as ranges. A single point estimate claims a precision the inputs do not support.

The share of value coming from the terminal assumption is stated explicitly.

The reproducibility test

The source ledger must let someone who has never seen your work rebuild the valuation.

This is the same standard the Week 7 deliverable is graded against. It is not a formality.

Negative results count

"I checked for revisions across these concepts and these filings and found none" is a complete finding.

"There were no revisions" without saying what you checked is not a finding, and will be treated as an unperformed check.

AI use

AI builds valuation models quickly and correctly. It will also invent plausible-looking inputs if you let it, will not know the vintage of anything, and will confidently produce a point estimate to two decimal places.

Never accept a number from an AI into your ledger without an independent source for it. A figure whose only provenance is that a model produced it is not sourced.

What must be yours: the company, the assumptions, the vintage choices, the sensitivity translation, and the range.

You must be able to explain every line of code and every input you submit.

Reproducibility and citation

Unchanged: fresh-kernel runs, every number traceable, every source in source_log.md with access route, coverage window, and retrieval date.
